FAQ

Do I need to pay?

No. Tickets are free — you choose them by notebooks: Standard = 1 notebook, VIP = 3 notebooks.

What does VIP get?

Front rows (after our guests), a bottle of water, a 10C9 buddy guide and the first turn on the Backstage Tour. Standard guests still join every activity and can complete the whole passport.

Where do the notebooks go?

All notebooks are counted publicly and handed to students in need through the school Youth Union, with a record signed by 3 people. Names and the number of notebooks of each person are not published.

I forgot my notebook!

Please tell the check-in desk — you can bring it later through your class monitor.

How do I complete the passport?

Open the Concert Passport tab and collect 6 stickers: 1 Check-in · 2 Sing & Fill (4/6) · 3 Museum Quiz (2/3) · 4–6 Stations A, B, C (both answers correct). 6/6 = COMPLETED. ★ Bonus is optional.

Photos

When you book, you choose whether we may take your photo. If you say no, you get a red dot on your passport and we will not use your photo.
