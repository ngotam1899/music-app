FAQ

Do I need to pay for a ticket?

No. All tickets are free. We kindly invite you to bring a donation instead.

What can I donate?

Books, clean clothes and school supplies in good condition.

How do I show my ticket?

Open "My Tickets" and show your e-ticket code at the entrance.

Can parents and teachers come?

Yes! Choose the "Family & Teachers" ticket type in the Ticketbox.
